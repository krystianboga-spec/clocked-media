## 2. Reusable formats extracted

1. Loading-then-reveal: a literal "loading" frame, then the payoff cut on the beat drop (row 1).
2. Then-and-now: one messy state, one clean state, a single wipe between (row 2).
3. Timed-line counter scene: two or three voices trade short lines, one shot per line, original audio and no song (row 4).
4. Talking object with notification: one object addresses the viewer, a phone notification drives the plot, SFX heavy (rows 4, 7).
5. High cut rate under one continuous sound for energy (row 5). Our engine can do about 1.5 to 2.5 s per shot, so we borrow the rhythm, not the 0.8 s rate.

## 3. What we borrow, what we must not

Borrow: structure (setup, beat, payoff), where the cut falls relative to the beat, shot length range, on-screen "loading" device, then-and-now contrast, line-by-line timed dialogue, notification-as-plot, serial naming, obvious stylisation.

Must not:
- No footage, characters, mascots, logos, scripts, voices or music from any example. That means no black clapping cat, no fruit cast, no STI monsters, no Tralalero-style Italian brainrot animals, no template names as titles ("Me At Work", "I Be Thinking Bout My Bills" must not appear in captions or titles).
- No trending commercial tracks (Tate McRae, Lord Huron, Diana Ross and similar). Business accounts often cannot use them anyway; use our own generated or licensed music.
- No real people, no celebrity or creator voices, no lookalike avatars.
- No "AI slop" photoreal look. Our hand-painted 2D look is the differentiator, and stylised-and-honest is what the best ad case (row 8) leaned into.
- Claims only: projected pay including 1.5x overtime after 40 hours a week, payday phone reminders, multiple jobs, and email import only as "building this next". No tips, tax calculation, net pay, user counts or reviews. Every figure is an example, adds up, and is before taxes.

## 4. Four original Clocked concepts

## 5. Sound and pacing patterns (what to carry into production)

1. Cut on the beat drop. The month's biggest formats put the reveal on the bass drop (Virlo, Oct 29, loading transition on "H4UNT" and the Paparazzi bow on the drop). Use one drop per video and build every card to it.
2. Voice or original audio carries the video, music stays secondary. Top Starrd templates run on original audio, timed lines or SFX only (Oct to Nov 2026). Timed line delivery plus clean SFX suits Kokoro.
3. One idea, short length, many small visual changes. Top AI templates run 8 to 19 s with 4 to 20 shots (Starrd). We use 16 to 24.5 s, shots 2.5 s or less, one number per card.
