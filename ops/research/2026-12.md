# Clocked video format research, 2026-12 (run date 2026-11-20)

Window: about 2026-10-07 to 2026-11-20. Method: WebSearch plus page reads (Firecrawl). Caveats:

- I could not watch any video. Mechanics below come from tracker and press descriptions, not frame counts.
- Template trackers (SameButMe, Virlo) report plays of the top example, not totals.
- Several rows are not AI-made. They are kept because the FORMAT is what we borrow. The two ad rows (5, 6) are the month's clearest signal about how AI-made ads land with audiences.

## 2. What we borrow, what we must not

Borrow: deadpan-then-reveal on a drop (1), same-answer escalation (2), tension build then calm release (3), refuse-then-give-in beat for the mascot (4), hand-made painted texture and small human moments (5, 6), short single-idea length (7).

Must not: any creator footage, frames, sounds, songs, script lines or characters; no template names or trend sound titles in captions; no real people or likenesses; no photoreal AI look. Music stays the engine's own synthesized beds.
